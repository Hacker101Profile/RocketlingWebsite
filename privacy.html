How a flight goes

In orbit

Circle your planet

Inside a planet's gravity ring you swing around it in a steady orbit for as long as you like. Burn forward to climb, burn backward to drop lower.

Escape

Break free

Push hard enough and you leave the ring. Out in deep space nothing pulls on you, so you coast in a straight line toward your next target.

Arrival

Reach a new world

Fly into another planet's gravity to reach it and refill your tank. Go all the way around it once for an orbit bonus.

Everything has a value

Every game builds a new galaxy of 110 planets, some close together and some a long coast away. Collect what floats between them, and watch your fuel: when the tank runs dry, the flight is over.

Common questions

How do I fly the rocket?

Hold ◀ or ▶ to turn and hold 🔥 to fire the engine. You can press several buttons at once. Tap PRO to point the rocket the way it's moving (to speed up) or RETRO to point it backwards (to slow down). Turning by hand switches auto-aim off.

What is the dotted line in front of my rocket?

It shows the path your rocket will follow if you stop thrusting. A red dot on the line means that path hits a planet.

How do I leave a planet's gravity?

Tap PRO and burn. Your orbit stretches further out with every second of thrust until it crosses the dashed ring around the planet. Time the burn so that you leave the ring heading toward the planet you want to reach. The green arrow points to the nearest planet you haven't reached yet.

How do I get more fuel?

Reaching a new planet refills your tank completely. Fuel cans (⛽️) add a quarter of a tank each. Crashing or tapping ↺ puts you back in orbit but does not refill fuel.

Why did my game end?

A flight ends when your fuel runs out. Tap the screen on the "Out of fuel" panel to start again in a new galaxy.

Does Rocketling need an internet connection?

No. The whole game runs on your device and works offline.

How do I reset my best score?

Your best score is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

Rocketling privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

In short: Rocketling does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game does not connect to the internet.

Who we are

Rocketling is developed and published by askkrat ("we", "us"). askkrat is responsible for this app and for this policy. You can reach us at akrat2720@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through Rocketling. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not access your camera or microphone.

Information stored on your device

Rocketling saves your best score on your device using the standard iOS app storage, so it is still there the next time you play. This value stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

  • Rocketling shows no advertising.
  • Rocketling contains no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • Rocketling does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • Rocketling does not make network connections.

Information handled by Apple

Rocketling is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes information about your download under Apple's own privacy policy. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you personally, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the game.

When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message. We use this only to answer you and help with your request. We do not share it with anyone, and we will delete your messages on request.

Children's privacy

Rocketling is suitable for players of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) may give you the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Since Rocketling does not collect personal data, we hold none from the app itself. If you have emailed us, you can ask us at any time to show you or delete that correspondence.
